LetFile for accountants

A simple, cheap tool to hand your landlord clients, especially the jointly-owned ones, so they stay compliant and happy without eating your margin.

For accountants and bookkeepers with UK landlord clients

The problem MTD just handed you

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax turns one annual return into five filings a year per client. For your straightforward landlords, that's a lot of low-value work. And for jointly-owned property it's worse: each co-owner is a separate taxpayer who files their own share, so a couple is ten filings and a fiddly apportionment of every figure.

Those simple joint-owner clients are rarely worth full-service fees, but you don't want to lose them, or spend your time hand-splitting rent four times a year.

What LetFile is

LetFile is a focused MTD filing tool built specifically for UK property, including jointly-owned property. The client enters the property and ownership split once; LetFile apportions every figure, files separately under each owner's own HMRC authorisation, handles Section 24 per owner, and submits the final declaration. It files directly to HMRC through the official MTD API.

  • £29 to £99 a year (Single £29, Couple £49 for both owners, Portfolio £99), not per month.
  • Does the joint-ownership split automatically, the thing generic tools ignore.
  • Free for the client to set up; they only pay when they file.

The honest bit: LetFile is currently individual-facing, there's no agent filing flow yet, so today you refer clients rather than file on their behalf through LetFile. An agent flow is on our roadmap; tell us if you'd use it and we'll prioritise it.

Why hand a client to LetFile

  • Keep the client, lose the chore. They stay yours for the work that matters; LetFile just handles their routine quarterly filing.
  • The joint-ownership split is done right, so you're not fielding "which half is mine?" questions or correcting apportionment errors.
  • It's cheap enough to be a no-brainer for the client, so recommending it makes you look good without costing them much.
  • You focus your time on advisory and the complex returns that actually pay.
