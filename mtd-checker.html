How the check works

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax is phased in by income. You're brought in when your qualifying income, your gross self-employment plus property income combined, before expenses, crosses the threshold for that year:

  • over £50,000, you're in from 6 April 2026;
  • over £30,000, from 6 April 2027;
  • over £20,000, from 6 April 2028.

It's your turnover, not your profit, so a landlord with £55,000 of rent and a big mortgage is still over the £50,000 line. Once you're in, you send four quarterly updates and a final declaration each year instead of one Self Assessment.

Own a property jointly? Your qualifying income is your share, so a couple splitting the rent are each measured on half. See MTD for jointly owned property and the full deadlines guide.
