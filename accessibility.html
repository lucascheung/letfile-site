Accessibility statement for LetFile

Applies to letfile.co.uk and the LetFile app at app.letfile.co.uk. Last reviewed 5 November 2026.

LetFile is run by Gojin Ltd. We want everyone to be able to file their taxes, so we build LetFile to be usable by as many people as possible, whatever their abilities or the technology they use.

How accessible this product is

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at AA level. We've self-assessed the product against those guidelines. In practice this means:

  • the pages are built from clear, semantic HTML with headings, landmarks and a skip link;
  • every form field has a label, with hints and clear, text-based error messages;
  • you can operate everything with a keyboard;
  • text colours meet AA contrast, and you can zoom to 200% or use a narrow screen without losing content;
  • tables have captions and proper headers, and live figures (like the per-owner split) are announced to screen readers;
  • colour is never the only way we show something, text labels always accompany it.

Known limitations

We're honest about where we're still improving:

  • on links and buttons we currently rely on the browser's default focus outline rather than a custom one;
  • not every interface component has been formally audited for non-text (3:1) contrast;
  • autocomplete is set on identity fields but not on every input.

We're working to close these.

Reporting an accessibility problem

If you find something that isn't accessible, or you need information in a different format, email hello@letfile.co.uk and we'll do our best to help and to fix it.

Enforcement

If you're not happy with how we respond, the Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) is responsible for enforcing the accessibility regulations. You can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS).
